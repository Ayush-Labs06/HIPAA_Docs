# Glossary (the words that trip people up)

Alphabetical. Plain words first, then the term. Each entry points to the module that covers it.

**Addressable.** A Security Rule spec you must assess. Implement it if reasonable and appropriate; if not, document why and use an equivalent. It does *not* mean optional. → [module 06](../06_Security_Rule/security_rule_explained.md)

**Affiliated covered entity (ACE).** Legally separate CEs under common ownership that designate themselves as one CE (§164.105(b)). → [module 01](../01_Scope_and_Roles/who_hipaa_applies_to.md)

**Authorization.** A patient's signed permission for a use or disclosure not otherwise allowed (§164.508). → [module 03](../03_Privacy_Rule/authorizations_and_special_permissions.md)

**BAA (business associate agreement).** The contract binding a BA to protect PHI (§164.504(e)). → [module 08](../08_Business_Associate_Agreements_and_Vendors/baa_clause_by_clause_review.md)

**Breach.** An impermissible use or disclosure of unsecured PHI that is *presumed* a breach unless a risk assessment shows a low probability of compromise (§164.402). → [module 07](../07_Breaches_and_Incidents/is_it_a_breach.md)

**Business associate (BA).** A person or company that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI for a CE, or provides certain services using it (§160.103). → [module 01](../01_Scope_and_Roles/business_associates_explained.md)

**CAP (corrective action plan).** The 2-3 year to-do list in an OCR settlement. → [module 14](../14_Compliance_Program_and_Audit/ocr_investigations_audits_and_questionnaires.md)

**CE (covered entity).** A health plan, health care clearinghouse, or health care provider that conducts standard electronic transactions (§160.103). → [module 01](../01_Scope_and_Roles/who_hipaa_applies_to.md)

**CMP (civil money penalty).** A per-violation fine from OCR, in four tiers by culpability. → [module 16](../16_Enforcement_and_Case_Law/ocr_penalties_and_how_enforcement_works.md)

**Conduit exception.** A narrow carve-out for entities that only transmit PHI, like the postal service or an ISP. → [module 01](../01_Scope_and_Roles/business_associates_explained.md)

**De-identified.** Data meeting §164.514(a)-(b), so no longer PHI. Two routes: Safe Harbor or Expert Determination. → [module 02](../02_PHI_and_Data_Mapping/de_identification_and_limited_data_sets.md)

**Designated record set (DRS).** Records a patient can ask to see or amend: medical and billing records, plus enrollment and case files used for decisions (§164.501). → [module 05](../05_Individual_Rights/right_of_access.md)

**DUA (data use agreement).** The contract required before sharing a limited data set (§164.514(e)). → [module 02](../02_PHI_and_Data_Mapping/de_identification_and_limited_data_sets.md)

**EHI (electronic health information).** ePHI that would be in a designated record set (45 CFR 171.102); the information-blocking term. → [module 15](../15_Related_Laws/information_blocking_and_interoperability.md)

**ePHI.** PHI in electronic form. The Security Rule applies only to this. → [module 02](../02_PHI_and_Data_Mapping/what_is_phi.md)

**Expert Determination.** De-identification by a qualified statistician who documents that re-identification risk is very small (§164.514(b)(1)). → [module 02](../02_PHI_and_Data_Mapping/de_identification_and_limited_data_sets.md)

**Four-factor assessment.** The breach risk assessment: nature of the PHI, who received it, whether it was viewed, how far mitigated (§164.402(2)). → [module 07](../07_Breaches_and_Incidents/is_it_a_breach.md)

**HBNR (Health Breach Notification Rule).** The FTC rule (16 CFR Part 318) for health apps outside HIPAA. → [module 11](../11_Tracking_Tech_and_Health_Apps/health_apps_and_non_hipaa_data.md)

**HITECH.** The 2009 law that made BAs directly liable and added breach notification. → [module 00](./01_what_hipaa_actually_is.md)

**HITRUST.** A private security framework (e1, i1, r2). It certifies a HITRUST assessment, not legal compliance. → [module 14](../14_Compliance_Program_and_Audit/framework_crosswalk.md)

**Hybrid entity.** An entity with covered and non-covered functions that designates its health care components (§164.103, §164.105). → [module 01](../01_Scope_and_Roles/who_hipaa_applies_to.md)

**IIHI (individually identifiable health information).** Health information that identifies, or could identify, a person. PHI is IIHI held by a CE or BA. → [module 02](../02_PHI_and_Data_Mapping/what_is_phi.md)

**Information blocking.** A practice likely to interfere with access, exchange or use of EHI (45 CFR Part 171). → [module 15](../15_Related_Laws/information_blocking_and_interoperability.md)

**LDS (limited data set).** PHI minus direct identifiers, keeping dates and some geography, usable for research, public health or operations under a DUA (§164.514(e)). → [module 02](../02_PHI_and_Data_Mapping/de_identification_and_limited_data_sets.md)

**Minimum necessary.** Use, disclose or request only the PHI needed (§164.502(b)). → [module 03](../03_Privacy_Rule/minimum_necessary.md)

**NPI (National Provider Identifier).** The 10-digit provider ID in standard transactions (Part 162). → [module 02](../02_PHI_and_Data_Mapping/what_is_phi.md)

**NPP (Notice of Privacy Practices).** The notice a CE gives patients on its uses and their rights (§164.520). → [module 04](../04_Notice_of_Privacy_Practices/notice_of_privacy_practices.md)

**OCR (Office for Civil Rights).** The HHS office that enforces HIPAA civilly. → [module 16](../16_Enforcement_and_Case_Law/ocr_penalties_and_how_enforcement_works.md)

**OHCA (organized health care arrangement).** A joint arrangement, like a hospital and its medical staff, sharing PHI for joint operations (§160.103). → [module 01](../01_Scope_and_Roles/who_hipaa_applies_to.md)

**Part 2.** 42 CFR Part 2, extra confidentiality rules for substance use disorder (SUD) treatment records. → [module 13](../13_Special_Contexts/substance_use_records_part_2.md)

**Personal representative.** Someone with authority to act for the patient in health decisions, such as a parent of a minor (§164.502(g)). → [module 13](../13_Special_Contexts/minors_representatives_and_sensitive_care.md)

**PHI (protected health information).** IIHI held by a CE or BA, in any form (§160.103). → [module 02](../02_PHI_and_Data_Mapping/what_is_phi.md)

**Psychotherapy notes.** A therapist's separate session notes; extra protection, usually need authorization (§164.501, §164.508(a)(2)). → [module 03](../03_Privacy_Rule/authorizations_and_special_permissions.md)

**QSOA (qualified service organization agreement).** The Part 2 version of a BAA. → [module 13](../13_Special_Contexts/substance_use_records_part_2.md)

**Recognized security practices.** Under Pub. L. 116-321 (2021), OCR must consider whether the entity had them (NIST CSF, 405(d)) for the prior 12 months when setting penalties. → [module 06](../06_Security_Rule/security_rule_explained.md)

**Required.** A Security Rule implementation spec you must do. → [module 06](../06_Security_Rule/security_rule_explained.md)

**Resolution agreement.** A settlement with OCR, usually with a payment and a CAP. → [module 16](../16_Enforcement_and_Case_Law/big_cases_and_lessons.md)

**Risk analysis.** An accurate, thorough assessment of risks to *all* ePHI (§164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A)). → [module 06](../06_Security_Rule/risk_analysis_with_worked_example.md)

**Safe Harbor.** De-identification by removing 18 listed identifiers with no actual knowledge of re-identification (§164.514(b)(2)). → [module 02](../02_PHI_and_Data_Mapping/de_identification_and_limited_data_sets.md)

**Sanctions.** Discipline for workforce members who break policy (§164.308(a)(1)(ii)(C), §164.530(e)). → [module 06](../06_Security_Rule/risk_analysis_with_worked_example.md)

**Subcontractor.** A person to whom a BA delegates a function involving PHI. It is itself a BA (§160.103). → [module 01](../01_Scope_and_Roles/business_associates_explained.md)

**TEFCA.** Trusted Exchange Framework and Common Agreement: a national health data exchange framework under the Cures Act. → [module 15](../15_Related_Laws/information_blocking_and_interoperability.md)

**TPO (treatment, payment, health care operations).** The three big purposes for which PHI may flow without authorization (§164.506). → [module 03](../03_Privacy_Rule/uses_and_disclosures_tpo.md)

**Unsecured PHI.** PHI not made unusable or unreadable per HHS guidance (valid encryption or destruction). → [module 07](../07_Breaches_and_Incidents/is_it_a_breach.md)

**Workforce.** Employees, volunteers, trainees and others under the entity's direct control (§160.103). → [module 01](../01_Scope_and_Roles/privacy_and_security_officers.md)

**X12.** The EDI formats (837, 835, 270/271) that are HIPAA's standard transactions. → [module 00](./02_meet_pulseledger.md)

## Try this

Pick five terms. Explain each to a non-technical friend in **one sentence**, with no jargon from this page. Example: "A business associate is a company that does paperwork or tech for a hospital, so it must protect patient information too." Ask your friend to repeat it back. If they cannot, rewrite it.
